We plan for **a week without power**, not the end of the world. Then we stretch it to two.

One night this week we sat down and did it for real: **four adults, two little kids** (the family across the street comes here), **and our dogs**. Here's the order we think in, with what we already had and what we're adding.

The order matters. In a cold-winter outage, what hurts you first is **cold, then water, then meds, then food.**

## 1. Heat

**What we already had**
- A [Mr. Heater Portable Buddy](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B002G51BZU?tag=homesteade0d4-20), a propane heater rated for indoor use.
- A pile of full 20-lb propane tanks from the last big blizzard.
- Firewood from the 8–10 trees we took down in the spring, plus the small stuff already stacked.

**What we're adding**
- A [Kidde battery carbon monoxide alarm](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B004Y6V5CI?tag=homesteade0d4-20). We had one in the kitchen. FEMA's *Are You Ready?* guide says carbon monoxide detectors belong on **every floor and outside the bedrooms**.
- A [General Tools moisture meter](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00275F5O2?tag=homesteade0d4-20). Wood cut in spring is often not dry yet. Split it, stack it off the ground, cover the top, and burn it when it reads under 20%.

**The rules**
- Never run a generator, grill or camp stove inside or in the garage.
- One room you can heat. Hang blankets over the doorways.

## 2. Water

Plan on **1 gallon per person per day**, more for little kids and anyone pregnant. Dogs need about 1 gallon for every 3 days (Mississippi State Extension).

**What we already had**
- 100 water purification tablets.
- [Membrane Solutions filter straws](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07T23BMPS?tag=homesteade0d4-20), good for a lot of gallons.
- One rain barrel hooked up, and an ornamental pond.

**What we're adding**
- Two [5-gallon water jugs with a spigot](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D1V9PGCG?tag=homesteade0d4-20).
- A [rain barrel diverter kit](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CY7GCW2F?tag=homesteade0d4-20) to cut the second barrel into a downspout. Close the valve before a hard freeze.

**The rules**
- Pond and rain water is for flushing, washing and animals. To drink it, filter it, then boil or treat it.
- Read your tablet box. Iodine-treated water is not recommended for pregnant women or anyone with thyroid problems, and nobody should drink it for more than a few weeks straight (Mississippi State Extension). Boil instead, or use chlorine dioxide tablets.
- When a storm is coming, fill the tub and every pot.

## 3. Meds and first aid

- Ask your doctor and pharmacy for a **90-day fill** before winter.
- A paper list of every med, dose and doctor, in a waterproof bag.
- A [336-piece first aid kit](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B083X6DJCL?tag=homesteade0d4-20) on top of what's in the cabinet.

## 4. Food

Eat the freezer first, then the fridge, then the pantry.

**What we already had**
- A big propane stove (six-plus burners) under the pergola that hooks right to a 20-lb tank. That's our kitchen when the power's out.
- A normal pantry. The kids eat regular food.

**What we're adding**
- [Augason Farms 30-day food kits](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FH3K7GS3?tag=homesteade0d4-20). Each is one person for 30 days. Five of them cover all six of us for about four weeks.
- [Powdered whole eggs](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08RF341KL?tag=homesteade0d4-20) and [Nido powdered milk](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D2LRWDF1?tag=homesteade0d4-20). Snow ice cream and hot cocoa for the kids.
- For the dogs: an extra bag of the food they already eat, [Nature's Recipe chicken](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0045VI034?tag=homesteade0d4-20). Same food, no tummy trouble mid-storm.

## 5. Power and light

**What we already had**
- A [Jackery Solar Generator 1000 v2 with a 200W panel](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D2L1G66J?tag=homesteade0d4-20). Phones, lights and the radio.
- A [FosPower hand-crank weather radio](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C6C23VKC?tag=homesteade0d4-20) (NOAA, AM/FM, flashlight, phone charger).
- One headlamp and one handheld lamp.

**What we're adding**
- A [rechargeable headlamp 2-pack](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B4KK73NB?tag=homesteade0d4-20) and a [4-pack of battery lanterns](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B08WWX5GTZ?tag=homesteade0d4-20). One light per person. FEMA says flashlights, not candles.
- An [Anker 20,000 mAh power bank](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CZ9LH53B?tag=homesteade0d4-20) for a pocket.

## 6. Sanitation

- A [portable toilet](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09S9SW2VJ?tag=homesteade0d4-20) and [waste bags](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B075BZ8NK3?tag=homesteade0d4-20) in case the water's out.
- [Unscented baby wipes](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C79B2BTH?tag=homesteade0d4-20). With no running water, everybody uses them.

## What we didn't buy

We almost bought a second heater, small propane cans, a camp stove, another radio and another water filter. Then we checked the garage. **Look at what you already own before you shop.** Our cart dropped by more than a third.
