The check valves that kept that line full all summer are the reason it has to come out. They hold water in it all winter too, and water that freezes in a fitting splits it.

## What broke

Two things snagged me all season, and both of them are the timer.

**The scroll wheel flips to hours.** The XD sets run time with a scroll wheel. Roll it past 59 minutes and it sometimes stops counting minutes and starts counting hours. On August 18 the app logged Zone 4, the raised beds, at nearly 900 minutes. That is fifteen hours, and the likeliest explanation is that wheel. **[ALEX: did the beds show it, or did you catch it? One line.]** Read the display before you walk away from it.

**WiFi starts quit after 30 seconds.** Start Zone 4 from the app over WiFi and the manifold shuts the water off after about 30 seconds. Start it from close by over Bluetooth and it runs the whole cycle. **[ALEX: how you found it, and whether the app shows the run as stopped or as a full run.]**

The plan from the build was to let the soil sensors drive Zone 4 on their own, and anything driving it remotely goes over WiFi. Until I know which starts actually run, an automated watering is a guess.
